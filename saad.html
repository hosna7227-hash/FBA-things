8. Hazrat Sa'd Ibn Abi Waqqas (RA)

سعد بن أبي وقاص (  ؓ  )

Hazrat Sa'd Ibn Abi Waqqas (RA) was one of the ten companions promised Paradise and among the earliest people to embrace Islam. He accepted Islam at a very young age (17 years old) and became one of the greatest military commanders and archers in Islamic history.

He was honoured with the titles Ar-Raami al-Awwal fi al-Islam (الرامي الأول في الإسلام), meaning "The First Archer in Islam" and Faris al-Islam (فارس الإسلام), meaning "The Knight of Islam." These titles reflected his extraordinary courage, military brilliance and unmatched skill in archery.

Hazrat Sa'd (RA) participated in nearly every major battle alongside Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. He was incredibly skilled at Archery. During the Battle of Uhud(أُحُد), he threw 1000+ Arrows, never ever ever missed any target. That day, he defended the Messenger of Allah with remarkable precision and bravery, shooting arrows continuously one after another while protecting the Prophet from enemy attacks.He defended the Prophet Muhammad with such precision that the Prophet ﷺ personally handed him arrows and declared,

"O Sa'd, shoot! May my father and mother be sacrificed for you."
— Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

This was one of the highest expressions of love and honour ever given by the Prophet to one of his companions, demonstrating Sa'd's unique position among the Sahabah.

Beyond his excellence in battle, Hazrat Sa'd (RA) became one of Islam's greatest commanders. During the caliphate of Umar Ibn Al-Khattab (RA), he led the Muslim army in the historic Battle of Al-Qadisiyyah (just at the age of 37), where the mighty Persian Empire was decisively defeated which was the super power at that time. This victory changed the course of world history and opened Persia to Islam.

He later founded the city of Kufa and served as its first governor, establishing strong administrative foundations for the rapidly expanding Islamic state.

Hazrat Sa'd (RA) was also blessed with a special honour. Prophet Muhammad ﷺ prayed for him, asking Allah to make his arrows accurate and his supplications accepted. Because of this blessed prayer, he became widely known as Mustajab ad-Da'wah (مستجاب الدعوات), meaning "The One Whose Supplications Are Answered."

"O Allah, make his aim accurate and answer his supplications."
— Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

Hazrat Sa'd Ibn Abi Waqqas (RA) devoted his entire life to Islam through faith, courage, wisdom and obedience to Allah. His remarkable achievements as a warrior, commander and devoted companion continue to inspire Muslims across the world.
