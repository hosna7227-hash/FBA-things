6. Hazrat Zubair Ibn Al-Awwam (RA)

الزبير بن العوام (  ؓ  )

Hazrat Zubair Ibn Al-Awwam (RA) was one of the ten companions promised Paradise and among the earliest Muslims to embrace Islam. He was the cousin of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and one of the most fearless warriors in Islamic history.

He was honoured with the title Hawari Rasulillah (حواري رسول الله), meaning "The Disciple of the Messenger of Allah." The Prophet ﷺ once declared:

"Every Prophet has a disciple, and my disciple is Zubair."
— Prophet Muhammad ﷺ
(Sahih al-Bukhari & Sahih Muslim)

Hazrat Zubair (RA) accepted Islam at a very young age. Because of his faith, he suffered severe torture from his uncle, who wrapped him inside a burning mat and filled it with smoke to force him to abandon Islam. However, Zubair (RA) remained steadfast and declared that he would never return to disbelief.

He earned the distinction of being the first Muslim to unsheathe his sword in defence of Islam. Throughout his life, he participated in every major battle alongside Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, including Badr, Uhud, Khandaq, Khaybar and many others, displaying exceptional bravery and unwavering loyalty.

During the conquest of Egypt, Hazrat Zubair (RA) demonstrated extraordinary courage by climbing the Fortress of Babylon and leading the Muslim forces toward victory. His fearless actions inspired the army and became one of the memorable moments in Islamic military history.

His body bore numerous scars from battles fought in the cause of Allah. Every wound testified to his lifelong sacrifice and devotion to protecting Islam and the Muslim community.

Besides his courage, Hazrat Zubair (RA) was also known for his generosity, honesty and deep trust in Allah. He spent his wealth in charitable causes and remained humble despite his high status among the companions.

He was blessed with immense wisdom, noble character and unwavering faith. His life stands as a timeless example of courage, sacrifice, loyalty and complete reliance upon Allah.
